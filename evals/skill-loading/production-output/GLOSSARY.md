# Saturday reading

A personal reading habit for quiet enjoyment, without a reading quota.

## Language

**Reading Session**:
Ten reading minutes with a paper book or short story immediately after Saturday breakfast at the kitchen table, reduced to five minutes in a Retry when attendance failed. Extra time is optional; a brief interruption may be followed by resuming the same sitting.

**Trial**:
Four Saturdays spent at home; away Saturdays are excluded, while disrupted at-home Saturdays count as misses.

**Success**:
At least three completed Reading Sessions during a Trial or Retry, together with overall enjoyment, assessed mentally.

**Miss**:
An at-home Saturday on which a Reading Session is not completed, including a disrupted Saturday or an abandoned sitting.

**Retry**:
The single additional four-at-home-Saturday attempt after an unsuccessful Trial, with the same Success threshold. Sessions shorten to five minutes if attendance failed, the book changes if enjoyment failed, and both adjustments apply if both failed.
