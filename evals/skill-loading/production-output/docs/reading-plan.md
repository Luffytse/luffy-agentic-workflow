# Saturday reading plan

## Settled decisions

- Purpose: quiet enjoyment, with no book or page quota.
- Read paper books and short stories only; switching is allowed.
- Read for ten reading minutes immediately after Saturday breakfast at the kitchen table; extra time is optional.
- Keep the phone silent in another room.
- Brief interruptions may be followed by resuming the same sitting. Count reading minutes only; an abandoned sitting is a miss.
- Keep the habit entirely personal, with no software, purchases, or involvement of other people.
- A Trial covers four Saturdays at home. Exclude away Saturdays; count disrupted at-home Saturdays as misses.
- Success requires at least three completed sessions and overall enjoyment, tracked mentally.
- If the Trial fails, retry once for another four at-home Saturdays, with the same three-session and enjoyment threshold.
- If attendance failed, use five-minute sessions during the Retry. If enjoyment failed, switch books. Combine both adjustments if both failed; an enjoyment-only Retry retains ten-minute sessions.
- Stop without guilt if the Retry fails; there is no further retry.

The reader lives alone and controls Saturday breakfast. No other constraints were specified.

## Confirmation

All decision branches are settled. The reader explicitly confirmed shared understanding of the complete plan and closed the interview.

These are reversible personal routine choices; no ADR is warranted.
